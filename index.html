FAQ

COMMON QUESTIONS

Is the free tier actually free — no hidden costs? +
Yes. Modules 00, 01, 02, 03, 04, 06, 07, 08 and 09 are completely free — 87 lessons — along with the IB LVN Rejection strategy, the RPBG Colour System indicator, the pre-market checklist and the Trader Archetype Assessment. No credit card required to join. Module 05 (Gamma Exposure & Institutional Flow) and Module 10 (Advanced Auction) are part of the paid Accelerator Program, which also includes live sessions, full playbook access, and direct coaching.
I've never traded before. Is this right for me? +
Yes — Module 0: The Starting Line was built specifically for complete beginners. It assumes zero prior knowledge. You'll cover financial markets, how to read a chart, candlesticks, basic terminology, and whether trading is even right for you — before a single futures lesson begins.
Will this curriculum make me profitable? +
Honest answer: no curriculum can guarantee profitability. This one will make you knowledgeable — you'll understand the instruments, the structure, the framework, and have a real executable strategy. Profitability requires screen time, live experience, and the emotional reality of real capital at risk. That gap is what the Accelerator Program and live coaching are designed to close.
What instruments does this cover? +
The curriculum focuses on ES (S&P 500 futures) and NQ (Nasdaq futures). These are the two instruments in the TarrierFuture playbook. The methodology and frameworks taught are applicable to other futures instruments, but the examples and setups are built specifically around ES and NQ.
Do I need any paid options data tools to follow the curriculum? +
No. You can follow the free modules with just a TradingView account (free tier). Module 05, part of the Accelerator Program, teaches how options data and GEX work and what they reveal about market structure. Live options data becomes more relevant in the live sessions, where real-time flow analysis is part of the workflow.
What is prop trading and do I need it? +
Prop trading (proprietary trading) allows you to trade a funded account provided by a firm — meaning you can trade significant capital without risking your own. Module 9 covers prop trading in full. It's not a requirement to benefit from this curriculum, but it is the path I personally use and recommend for traders who want to scale without large personal capital.
When does the Accelerator Program launch? +
The Accelerator Program is in development alongside the free modules. Join the free community now to be first notified when it launches — members who join early will receive a founding member rate. The free modules will always remain free.
